About the Forum

A Gathering of Oyo State's Gaming Industry

The Oyo State Gaming and Lottery Board welcomes regulators, operators, licensees and industry stakeholders to a two-day forum dedicated to shaping a gaming industry that is well-regulated, fully compliant, and centred on player care — under the theme "Governing for Growth."

Interactive Forum

Candid panel sessions on regulation, compliance and industry growth.

Player Care Focus

A dedicated presentation on what player protection really means.

Award Night

Recognising excellence and responsible practice across the industry.

Networking

Connect with regulators, operators and stakeholders shaping the industry.

Good to Know

Guest Information

Important details to help you prepare.

Is this event open to the public?

No. This is a strictly by-invitation event. There are no tickets for sale — attendance is reserved for invited stakeholders, licensees and guests of the Oyo State Gaming and Lottery Board.

What is the dress code?

Day 1 (Welcome cocktail party) is smart casual. Day 2 Forum is corporate/business attire, while the Gala Dinner & Award Night in the evening is formal or traditional elegance.

Do I need to confirm my attendance?

Yes. If you have received an invitation, please confirm your availability using the form above so the Secretariat can plan seating, catering and materials accordingly.

What time should I arrive on Day 2?

Registration opens at 10:00 AM. We advise guests to arrive promptly, as the opening ceremony begins shortly after registration.

Who do I contact for assistance?

For special assistance, accommodation support, or general enquiries, please reach the Event Secretariat via the phone or WhatsApp details in the footer below.
